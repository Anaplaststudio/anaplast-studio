# Contributing

Contributions to code, synthetic testing, documentation, Arabic terminology and installation support are welcome. Report the Blender version, operating system, add-on version, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Use synthetic models only. Do not upload patient scans, photographs, names, medical record numbers or identifiable findings reports.

Keep changes focused and explain how you tested them. Do not present numerical defaults, geometric checks or generated airway channels as clinically validated. Preserve third-party notices and license terms. In particular, Mac and Linux installer testing is still needed.

Submit code contributions under the project's GPL-3.0-or-later license. Contact support@anaplaststudio.com for private support or concerns that should not be posted publicly.
