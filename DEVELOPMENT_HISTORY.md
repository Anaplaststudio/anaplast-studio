## Historical notes through v0.61.0

## 0.61.0 skin-following land, flush wedge ends, and ocular shells

- Follow skin height defaults to 100%: the outer land follows broad changes around the skin boundary, while easing outward without copying fine texture. Set it to zero to restore one outer level.
- Bullet wedges reach beyond the mold rim and are trimmed to its exact boundary. Their wide ends are flush with the edge rather than inset behind an end wall.
- The Ocular Shell panel uses Mirror_FaceScan by default. Trace the eyelid opening from the intended front view, clicking at least six ordered points; Enter finishes, Backspace removes a point, Esc cancels. The outline is extended by 3 mm in that view, adjustable.
- The exposed eye uses scanned heights with adjustable detail retention. A fitted convex surface continues beneath the lids. Corneal center, diameter, projection, and a visual iris diameter guide are adjustable. Missing corneal geometry is modelled, not recovered from the scan. Projection starts at zero.
- Ocular thickness starts at an editable 1 mm normal offset. The inner shell is not an impression-derived fitting surface. The build checks closure and self-intersection and preserves a previous result on failure. Impression/conformer matching is intentionally deferred.
- A separate Windows PNG brush installer creates anchored Draw brush assets from the user's PNGs, packs their images, renders surface previews, and maps input folders to Textures/subfolder catalogs. It does not include texture PNGs. Libraries are stored outside the add-on and backed up on update.

## 0.60.0 gentle land, experimental breakable cap, and generic substructure

- The land now uses a harmonic extension with a gradual fade to its flat rim. The new transition-width default is 14 mm; existing cases retain their saved width.
- Shell mode can use a separate experimental cap thickness down to 0.1 mm. Radial grooves, crossed grooves, edge starters, or no grooves are available. Grooves are cut from the outside and retain a nominal sealed floor. Keys remain thin-backed; spillways, ring, pry slots, bolts, and a reinforced rim are omitted in breakable mode.
- Thin caps use a 0.07 mm distance grid at the 0.1 mm setting, with local backing repair and final closure/intersection checks. Thickness is nominal and varies at sharp details. This setting is below the published Formlabs Form 4 Grey Resin supported-wall guideline of 0.2 mm; test printing on the actual printer/resin is required.
- The generic substructure fits inside the aligned outer prosthesis with default 3 mm silicone cover and 1.5 mm fitting relief. It smooths a working volume, checks both clearances, and preserves the previous result if no usable space remains. No structural minimum thickness is imposed.
- Component markers and adjustable cylindrical spaces support manual analog/magnet fitting. These are generic envelopes, not Southern IE or Technovent connection geometry. Exact component dimensions and positions must be supplied by the user.
- A Windows executable installer detects supported Blender installations, backs up older add-on copies in the selected profile, installs the embedded update, and enables it. Close Blender before running it.

Printer guidance: https://formlabs.com/white-papers/form-4-design-guide/


## 0.59.0 land, fitting accuracy, and controls

- A single flat outer-land plane, connected to the skin band by an adjustable transition. Feature paths use continuous height interpolation.
- Optional skin-band and base-fitting smoothing, both zero by default. A working copy is smoothed; the original scan is not edited. The cap sculpt has no smoothing control.
- Closed scans are used directly for fitting. Closed sculpt cutters without self-intersections retain the original surface without voxel resampling or decimation. Invalid, open, or self-intersecting cutters require repair at the chosen mold resolution, recorded in the build steps. This geometric repair is separate from optional smoothing, and its surface deviation must be measured.
- Pry-point width, inward depth, and opening height are adjustable.
- Bullet-shaped wedge noses begin outside the ring spillway, with adjustable clearance (default 1 mm) and nominal inner/outer groove diameters. The positive is reduced by the existing key-clearance setting.
- The finished cavity and flat land are retained through shell construction. The backing still uses the distance-based method introduced in 0.58.1.

The accompanying installer backs up matching add-on copies in the current Blender profile, installs this version, and enables it. Case files are not removed. Restart Blender after installation.

## 0.58.1 shell perimeter correction

The two-part mold's Shell option now removes the original block walls and end
faces before remeshing, using the actual trimming planes. Previously, remeshing
rounded those edges before a face-angle filter tried to remove them, leaving
fragments that grew into fins when shell thickness was added.

The backing is now constructed by a signed-distance offset behind the inner
surface, rather than by extruding faces that can fold over one another. A
temporary extended reference prevents enclosing walls or a flat base from being
left behind. The final difference retains the original cavity and straight
perimeter planes; the finished shell is not remeshed again. Mirror/source
selection, opening direction, registration keys, spillways, and the Full option
retain their existing behavior. Backing resolution is normally twice the mold
voxel size (0.30 mm for a 0.15 mm setting), capped at one-sixth of the shell wall
before the existing memory budget is applied. Thickness is approximate at tight
curves and narrow features. This version requires Blender 5.2 or newer for its
distance-grid tools.

This correction was exercised on Blender 5.2.1 LTS with the supplied case and
flat/curved shell tests at 2, 4, and 6 mm. Historical notes below describe earlier
versions and may not describe the current mold-building implementation.

Blender 5.2+ extension. This update was tested on Blender 5.2.1 LTS.

## Solids
Scans stay as scanned â€” the outer surface is the truth and nothing resamples it. Every operation that needs a solid tissue makes its own temporary, clipped, budgeted copy. If you want a permanent closed twin for manual booleans in Blender, tick **Make solid twin on import**: a hidden `<name>_Solid` is created at full resolution (outer surface untouched, thickened into the head). Note the honest limit: no remesh can make a 3-million-face scan a solid *and* boolean-friendly at full fidelity â€” a full-fidelity solid is 6 million faces, and that is what crashes the solver. That is why the cap / fitted pathways don't use booleans at all.

## Fidelity
Nothing in the pipeline reduces the prosthesis mesh unless you ask: the cap and fitted prosthesis copy the outer surface vertex-for-vertex (verified: 0.0000 mm change), the tissue-side surface is sampled at the prosthesis's own resolution, and *Simplify prosthesis for mold booleans* is OFF by default (big prostheses use the Fast solver instead of being decimated). The only decimation is on the temporary tissue copies used for booleans, which never touch your objects. **Fidelity Report** in the Case panel lists face count and edge length of every case object so you can see where resolution changes, if anywhere.

Two things that *look* like lost detail but aren't: flat vs smooth shading (right-click â†’ Shade Smooth / Shade Flat; generated meshes now inherit the source's setting), and Blender's viewport skipping detail on very dense meshes when zoomed out.
